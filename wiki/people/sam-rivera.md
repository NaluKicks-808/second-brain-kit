# Sam Rivera

Owner and lead mechanic of [[riverside-cycle-works]], a two-person bike shop. A fictional person, invented so that this kit ships with a working example; delete this page once you have a real one.

## How Sam decides

Sam refuses to give a customer a promised date at intake, and will only commit once the bike is on the stand and the fault is known. The consequence is visible in the shop's own records: the repair queue lives on a clipboard with nineteen tickets on it while the booking software, which demands a date before it will accept a job, shows four ([[2026-09-12_shop-visit-notes#^intake-backlog]]). The clipboard is not disorganisation. It is the only surface that lets a job exist without a promise attached to it.

The same reasoning sets the prices. Labour is billed in half-hour blocks, the parts markup is a flat rate whose origin Sam cannot recall, and the winter tune-up is the one item with a posted price ([[2026-09-12_shop-visit-notes#^pricing-blocks]]). Sam reads a posted price as a promise about time rather than about money, which is why the rest of the board is blank: no two repairs take the same hour, so no two can honestly carry the same number.

## Capacity

Sam hired a third mechanic for one summer and let the arrangement lapse, summarising it as the bench only fitting two ([[2026-09-12_shop-visit-notes#^bench-fits-two]]). The constraint Sam names is physical space rather than demand or wages.
