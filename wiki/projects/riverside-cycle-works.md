# Riverside Cycle Works

A two-person neighbourhood bike shop run by [[sam-rivera]], carrying a repair backlog it has never been able to price. A fictional business, invented so that this kit ships with a working example; delete this page once you have a real one.

## What it is

Repairs and servicing, walk-in and by appointment, with one part-time mechanic on weekends. Intake happens on a clipboard beside the workstand rather than in the shop's booking software, which is why the two disagree so loudly: nineteen open tickets on the clipboard against four in the software on the day of the visit ([[2026-09-12_shop-visit-notes#^intake-backlog]]). The software insists on a promised date at intake and the shop's owner will not give one before seeing the bike, so most jobs never enter it at all.

## How it charges

Labour in half-hour blocks, unchanged for two years. A flat markup on parts. One posted price in the whole shop, for the winter tune-up package ([[2026-09-12_shop-visit-notes#^pricing-blocks]]). The reasoning behind the blank price board is recorded on [[sam-rivera]]: a posted price reads as a promise about time, and no two repairs take the same hour.

## The live constraint

Capacity is described in terms of the bench rather than of demand or wages, a third mechanic was tried for one summer and the arrangement lapsed ([[2026-09-12_shop-visit-notes#^bench-fits-two]]). Any change to the backlog therefore runs into a physical limit before it runs into a hiring decision.

## What is not known

The cost of the backlog. The source puts no number on the work the shop turns away while tickets wait, and nothing here estimates one. It is carried as an open question on [[sam-rivera]] rather than filled in, because blank beats wrong.
